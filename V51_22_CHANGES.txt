Console College 26 v51.22 — Customize Your Universe

New Game now includes a per-save Customize Your Universe screen.

Toggles:
- Inbox & decisions
- Conference realignment
- Violations & compliance
- Player personalities
- Morale & portal drama
- Dynamic weather
- Injuries
- Coach carousel & firings
- NIL & booster pressure
- Media obligations

Includes Balanced, Chaos and Stable presets. Choices are stored on the save; old saves default to all systems enabled. Disabled systems are gated at their runtime entry points rather than merely hidden in the UI.
