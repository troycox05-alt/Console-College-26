Console College 26 — v53.01 · Game Day, all app
===============================================
* Your call is an app panel: down, distance and spot, timeouts, tempo, scheme and weather; big Run /
  Pass buttons and a "Staff's call: …" button; on fourth down Punt / Field goal (with the distance),
  the fakes with their odds and what the chart says; chips for kneel, timeout, the three tempos, sim
  ahead, substitutions and orders.
* Defense: the staff's call button and every defensive call as a tappable card (★ = your scheme's
  playbook, the staff's pick outlined), plus timeout / sim ahead / subs / orders / crowd / hand it to the DC.
* Run and pass play lists are grids of tappable plays.
* The last play's result sits right under the field. The booth (play-by-play and analyst) streams in
  as a chat, quarter breaks are banners, and every in-game choice (how to play it, speed, who calls
  plays, the try, sim-ahead, between-quarters) is a set of buttons.
* The game's own text is tucked into "Full broadcast text" at the bottom; the duplicate key row is
  hidden on game screens.
